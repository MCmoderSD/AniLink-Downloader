# Surface brief: the whole web frontend

Scope: every screen of the Angular app (Downloads, New download, 404, dialogs). Mode: operate.

Audience and task: self-hosters who paste the links of a season, then glance at progress and answer a password question, on a desktop and now also on a phone. Constraints from the owner: dark only, the cyan brand colour and the unchanged mark, product copy and behaviour untouched. What reads as unprofessional today: pills and rounded shapes everywhere, the stock Material look, small and dense type. Standard path chosen: the category standard played straight, at the craft level of the Vercel dashboard.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm dark dashboard in the category's own grammar: black page, hairline panels, square-ish controls and one signal colour, with nothing that asks to be looked at. It refuses the pill-shaped, tinted-cyan console and the stock Material component look.

OWN-WORLD: Page #000000, panels #0a0a0a, rows #111111, tags #1a1a1a, hairline #2e2e2e, field outline #6b6b6b, text #ededed and #a1a1a1. Cyan #0db7ed with ink #00131a only for the one action, the progress fill, focus and the selected option; green, amber and red only as status with a word beside them. Geist and Geist Mono at 400, 500 and 600. Corners of 4, 6, 8 and 12 pixels, full pills only for switches and scroll bars. Status shown as a small light in front of the word, never as a side stripe. Bars 6 pixels high.

STORY: The visitor sees at a glance which downloads run, which wait for a password and which failed, trusts the page because it is precise and quiet, and acts on the one filled button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Downloads at 1440 by 900: a 64 pixel bar with the mark and AniLink on the left and the plain Downloads button and the filled New download on the right; a 32 pixel page title with one counts line under it; below it stacked job panels of 8 pixel corners and a hairline, each with a 20 pixel name, a status tag with its light, two 6 pixel bars, a meta line with the path in monospace and one row of 40 pixel buttons of which only the first is filled.

FORM: the category standard (the standing exit), chosen by the owner; position: not ranked; seed: ab11cd13cb897162.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
